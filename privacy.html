Privacy Policy

Garden Fit · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what data the Garden Fit game uses, why, and what your rights are. The game is made by Dan Az Games Studio ("we"), the data controller under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and other applicable laws.

1. Summary

  • The game does not ask for an account, name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • We do not sell personal data or share it for marketing.
  • The game may be used by children. That is why all ads are non-personalized, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

2. Data kept on your device

The game stores on your device your progress (levels, stars, score, seeds, Collection items, postcards), the daily helpers and your settings (sound, language, style). This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game or clear the app's data, it is deleted.

3. Ads

To stay free, the game may show ads from Google AdMob. Ads follow the Google Play Families program rules:

  • all ads are non-personalized (your history is not used to choose them);
  • we only use ad providers certified for child audiences;
  • rewarded ads are always optional: the player chooses whether to watch.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process technical device data such as the advertising ID (where the law allows), IP address, device model, operating system and ad interactions. This processing follows the Google Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before showing ads, using Google's consent tool.

4. In-game purchases

If the game offers purchases, they are made through Google Play. Google handles the payment data; we do not receive your card number or billing details. We only receive the purchase confirmation, to unlock the item.

5. Device permissions

The game uses internet access (for ads and purchases) and vibration (it can be turned off in Settings). It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not ask anyone for personal data, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children, in line with the LGPD, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Ads are non-personalized for every player. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's data was collected, contact us below and we will delete it.

7. Legal bases

Technical data used to show ads and prevent fraud is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and safe and, where the law requires it, your consent. Purchase data is processed to perform the purchase contract.

8. Retention

Progress data stays on your device while the game is installed. Data processed by Google follows the retention periods in Google's policy.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask for confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion and portability, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not keep personal data on our own servers, the most direct way to delete your game data is to clear the app's data or uninstall it. For data processed by Google, use the options at myaccount.google.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Important changes will also be announced in the game.
